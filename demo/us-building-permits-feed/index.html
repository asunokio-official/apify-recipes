Honest limits

  • Three cities only: San Francisco, Austin and New Orleans (sources with an explicit public-domain or CC0 licence).
  • Not real time: permits usually appear 1 to 3 days after issue.
  • No owner, applicant or personal contractor names, no phones, no e-mails. Declared values are self-reported.

Ready-made inputs

New solar and roofing permits in Austin, TX
For: Solar installers, roofers and suppliers who track local demand and competitors' activity. Schedule: daily or weekly (use lookbackDays 7 for weekly). Max cost per run: $0.60005. 
{
  "cities": [
    "austin"
  ],
  "keywords": [
    "solar",
    "photovoltaic",
    "roof"
  ],
  "lookbackDays": 7,
  "onlyNew": true,
  "maxItems": 300
}
Run it: node node/src/cli.js run austin-solar-roof-permits --dry-run (free preview), then with APIFY_TOKEN and --yes.
