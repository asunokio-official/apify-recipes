Honest limits

  • Checks the websites you give it; it does not find new companies or build lead lists.
  • Unknown means not detected, never not used: tools loaded after consent, at checkout or by inline loaders can be missed.
  • Blocked, robots.txt-skipped and failed websites are returned with the reason and are free; no bypass is attempted.
