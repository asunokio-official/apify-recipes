UK Public Tenders Feed (Find a Tender + Contracts Finder)

Get new UK public-sector tenders and contract awards from Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, filtered by CPV code, region and value, delivered once.

Open asunoki/uk-public-tenders-feed on Apify

Price (pay per event): $0.004 per new notice stored, plus a $0.00005 run-start fee. Rows you filter out or already received are free; maxItems caps every run. Prices checked 2026-11-22; the Pricing tab on Apify is authoritative.

Every run also writes one free summary row, e.g. scanned 1102, matched 869, charged 15, stopped by max_items.

Honest limits

  • Find a Tender and Contracts Finder only; notices only on Public Contracts Scotland, Sell2Wales or eTendersNI are not included.
  • About a third of notices have no value; values are as published, currencies are not converted.
  • No contact officer names, e-mails or phones. Tender documents are not downloaded.

Who won construction contracts in Scotland (last 7 days)

{
  "noticeTypes": [
    "award"
  ],
  "cpvPrefixes": [
    "45"
  ],
  "regions": [
    "UKM"
  ],
  "days": 7,
  "onlyNew": false,
  "maxItems": 200
}
