New NPI Providers Feed (CMS NPPES weekly files)

Get the healthcare providers and organizations that received a new NPI last week, filtered by state, category or taxonomy code, delivered once.

Open asunoki/nppes-new-providers-feed on Apify

Price (pay per event): $0.002 per new provider stored, plus a $0.00005 run-start fee. Rows you filter out or already received are free; maxItems caps every run. Prices checked 2026-11-22; the Pricing tab on Apify is authoritative.

Honest limits

  • Weekly source: CMS publishes one file per week, about 1 to 2 days after the week ends. Only the two latest weeks exist, so schedule at least every 2 weeks.
  • A new NPI is an ID, not a licence, credential, Medicare enrolment or proof that a practice opened.
  • Individuals' street address and phone are off by default; no e-mail addresses exist in the source.

Ready-made inputs

Weekly new NPIs in Texas for dental practices
For: Dental supply, dental billing/RCM and practice-software sales teams who call on new dental practices. Schedule: weekly (Tuesday or Wednesday, after CMS posts the Monday-to-Sunday file). Max cost per run: $0.60005. 
{
  "states": [
    "TX"
  ],
  "categories": [
    "dental"
  ],
  "excludeSubparts": true,
  "weeks": 2,
  "onlyNew": true,
  "maxItems": 300
}
Run it: node node/src/cli.js run tx-dental-new-npis-weekly --dry-run (free preview), then with APIFY_TOKEN and --yes.
